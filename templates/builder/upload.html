    How the model is built

    The Summary PDF is the authority everything reconciles to.

    1. Every revenue and fee line is read from the monthly Summary PDFs.
    2. Transaction CSVs add units per SKU and cross-check each fee both ways.
    3. Amazon fees and PPC are split ex-VAT / VAT at 20%.
    4. You supply the landed cost — it appears in no Amazon export.
    5. Net Sales must equal Amazon's own Income total in every month, or the build tells you which month is off and by how much.
